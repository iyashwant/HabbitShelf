HabitKit screen gallery

Eight original website slideshow assets, followed by the additional product/help illustrations. Open any image for its original resolution. Observations describe what is visible; functional flows come from the documentation.

Start here · Feature checklist

01. Widgets

Single-habit contribution grids and a compact multi-habit widget.

Flow: Widget selection, completion action and midnight refresh.

Build note: WidgetKit plus App Intents and shared app storage.

02. Compact overview

Dense rows: colored leading icon, title and recent-day squares.

Flow: Tap today or a recent date; open detail separately.

Build note: Accessible targets around small squares; consistent day-key calculations.

03. Habit detail

Large grid, goal/streak chips and a navigable monthly calendar.

Flow: Edit past days, open day notes and inspect the habit.

Build note: One history model should feed calendar and heatmap.

04. Habit editor

Icon area, name/description fields and a palette.

Flow: Select identity; configure build/quit, tracking and goals.

Build note: Keep creation short and optional controls progressive.

05. Statistics

Year navigation, metric cards and a monthly completions chart.

Flow: Choose year/habits and inspect progress.

Build note: Specify completion-rate denominator before implementation.

06. Card overview / goals

Stacked cards with full grids and colored schedule chips.

Flow: Quick check-in, detail navigation and context actions.

Build note: Virtualize/cache grids when many habits are present.

07. Share card

Image preview, light/dark toggle, palette and share button.

Flow: Configure image appearance and open system sharing.

Build note: Generate original artwork using an independent app identity.

08. Month overview

Three-column collection of mini monthly calendars.

Flow: Browse months and check progress across many habits.

Build note: Handle narrow screens, Dynamic Type and large habit collections.
