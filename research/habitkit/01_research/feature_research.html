HabitKit feature research

A public-source reference for building an independent iOS habit tracker. Baseline: website help for 1.18.0, with later-version privacy notes called out separately.

Inspected 33 unique product/help pages and collected 45 public image assets. This is website research; the installed app was not exercised. Functional requirements below are documented behavior. Build phases are our proposal.

Product loop

Create a habit, record an action or amount, and see its calendar tile change. The contribution grid makes cumulative consistency visible. Users can keep the interaction to one check-in or add goals, reminders, notes, charts and widgets.

Current limits

  • No automatic live sync between devices, including Pro.
  • No dedicated Apple Watch app, complication or iPhone Lock Screen widget.
  • No native iPad layout documented; iPhone compatibility mode is described.
  • No custom day rollover time; local midnight is the boundary.
  • No streak-freeze token, named skip day or fixed-weekday streak schedule.
  • Quit habits have daily limits, not weekly/monthly budgets.
  • No built-in CSV import/export or migration from other habit trackers.
  • No individual quit-habit completion charts.
  • The public site does not expose app source code, database internals or complete acceptance rules.

Version and price context

The US App Store page showed version 1.18.0, dated September 21, and requires iOS 15+. Store product listings include multiple offers; they are not proof of the current paywall price. The new app’s deployment target, pricing and free limits remain your product choices.
