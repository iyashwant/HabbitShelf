# UI decisions

Reading this as a native daily habit tracker for a person checking in between activities, with HabitKit's compact dark contribution-grid language. The public app images are the visual authority. Design variance 2, motion intensity 2, visual density 7: preserve the established layout and quick interactions.

## Reference and tokens

Primary reference: the owner's own HabitKit dark-mode screenshots (cards, compact, detail, New Habit, Settings; 1179 × 2556, captured 2026-10-03), then `research/habitkit/02_app_screenshots/`. Tokens were sampled from those images after converting the two Display P3 captures to sRGB: canvas `#101013`, habit-card base `#1A1A1D` with a 5% habit-color tint fading out by 65% height, flat panels `#161619`, fields `#19191C`, round controls `#2B2B30` with a `#3B3B40` hairline, violet actions `#8B8AF8`, and the 21 Tailwind-400 habit colors. Empty tiles use 9% of the habit color inside the tracked range and 4.7% outside it (18% / 12% in the detail history). Light mode keeps the same structure on a `#F6F6F8` canvas with white cards.

Text uses bundled Figtree (SIL OFL), identified from the reference glyphs; dates, counts and small uppercase labels use SF Mono. Icons are bundled Lucide outlines (ISC), the reference's icon set; the chart, completion check, month-picker calendar and view-switcher glyphs are drawn from measured reference geometry where they differ from Lucide. Existing Font Awesome habit icons still render and remain searchable. Custom fonts scale with Dynamic Type, which rounds them to whole points, so a few labels use small tracking adjustments to keep reference widths. Contribution tiles carry progress, partial fill, today's outline and slip markers. Calendar cells offer explicit editing and accessible day labels; the heatmap is a summary rather than tiny tappable pixels.

The signature appears in card contribution grids, detail history, month mini-grids, share cards and home-screen widgets. Habit identity owns its color; global navigation and Save use the accent, violet unless the owner picks another in Theme settings. Navigation follows the reference's settings / statistics / plus toolbar and bottom three-view selector. Card rows have 14-point padding and 20-point corners; controls use smaller radii and primary actions use capsules.

## Component checkpoints

| Component | Intent and hierarchy | Surface and type | Spacing / states |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overview | Check in quickly; title and completion control lead the grid | Dark cards, native headline, muted description; per-habit accent | 16-point card inset; explicit empty state, error recovery, archive menu |
| Compact rows | Compare recent dates; name plus independent day controls | Shared day tile and habit icon; "Last N days" pill and uppercase SF Mono period | 52-point rows, 30-point tiles at a 38-point pitch; future and paused dates disabled, pre-start dates tappable for backfill; accessible amount/date labels; the pill opens Compact List Settings |
| Editor | Name and identity first; progressive numeric/cadence controls | Native segmented pickers, text input, 21-color palette | Grouped 20-point screen inset; draft preserved on failed save |
| Calendar | Correct a date or add a note | Native month controls; outlined today; slip/check markers | Seven columns; system sheet and Cancel/Save |
| Statistics | Read totals, yearly history and monthly consistency | Two metric columns and native Charts; selected habit accent | Native scroll and selection; textual monthly alternative to chart |
| Sharing | Preview the image before sharing | Actual SwiftUI view rendered as PNG; theme/color controls | Native system share; errors stay visible; no simulated sharing success |
| Settings | Manage data and reminders deliberately | APP / HELP / ABOUT groups of 53-point rows with tinted icon tiles; each row opens a page | Theme page offers System / Light / Dark (new installs start dark) and the accent color; import validation before confirmation; recoverable replacement; denied-permission path |

## Skill application

Frontend Design: follow the concrete brief and deliberate hierarchy, rather than inventing a new aesthetic. UI/UX Pro Max: native SwiftUI accessibility search returned Dynamic Type, semantic views and descriptive labels; its broad design-system search initially suggested off-brief marketing/clay styling, so that output was discarded. The narrower retry supported flat mobile UI, but the supplied reference still controls palette and typography. Interface Design: reusable tokens, surface depth, explicit states and component checkpoints. Taste: brief inference, shape consistency and restrained motion; its native-mobile exclusion means web-specific typography/packages/hero rules do not apply here.

## Dashboard, streak and year views

Added at the owner's request from five reference images of a light, pastel tracker (Today tiles and list, a widget board, yearly cards and a 12-month year page). Existing screens are unchanged; the new views use the same tokens, Figtree and SF symbols for flames and checks.

- Overview layouts **Streaks** (two-column tiles: streak count with unit, today's check, name, lettered week strip with done/7) and **List** (one row per habit) join Cards, Compact and Month in the view switcher. Both use the tinted style: pastel washes in light mode, tinted dark surfaces in dark mode.
- **Dashboard** opens from the overview toolbar (the reference's PRO slot). Small widgets take half a row, medium ones a full row and large ones a full row at double height, like the home screen. The pencil-style control edits order, removal and reset; plus adds a widget with a live preview. Add Widget keeps the designs compact: three tabs (One Habit, Several Habits, Routine), each a page of design tiles three to a row that lists their sizes (S · M · L); swiping sideways pages between the tabs without changing the chosen design; then size, the habit, habits or routine, a title for multi-habit designs, Auto / Light / Dark / Tinted style and a color (the habit's own by default). Touch and hold a widget to change its size or style, duplicate, edit or remove it. Tapping a widget opens its habit (the badge opens Milestones, the week the Weekly Recap, a routine its habits); checks log today and a routine's Complete All finishes it.
- Designs beyond the original five: **Goal Ring** (today's amount toward a numeric goal, or this week's days for a yes/no goal), **Stats** (kept share of 30 days, streak, best run and total; medium adds the 30 days as bars), **This Year** (a week-column contribution grid, or twelve month grids when large, with streak, longest run and this month), **Next Badge** (a ring toward the next milestone), **Quick Check** (habit icons that log with a tap, 4 / 8 / 16, rows centered when fewer), **This Week** (kept share per day as bars, against last week), **Routine** (count, per-habit segments and Complete All; medium lists its habits), plus a large **This Month** with numbered days, a medium **Today Ring** listing what's left and a medium **Today List**. Multi-habit designs keep each habit's color; the widget color tints the rest.
- **Year History** (one card per year, 21 days to a row, longest streak and completion) and **Year Calendar** (twelve month grids with stats, shareable as a PNG) open from the habit detail's options menu.
- Every design is also a home-screen widget in the same sizes, with interactive checks and Complete All. Home-screen widgets take their habit, habits or routine and theme from Edit Widget and keep the habit's color.

Verified on the simulator in both themes with the ten-habit sample fixture; home-screen placement of the new widgets still needs a device check.

## Explore pages

A compass button beside Dashboard opens **Explore**, a two-column grid of tinted cards, each with a live one-line summary: Insights, Calendar, Weekly Recap, Notes, Milestones, Routines and Timer. Each page uses the detail screen's back control and centered title, flat panels with uppercase captions, and the habit colors for data. Sentences lead each Insights section ("You're most consistent on Mondays (92%)"), with a chart beneath; within five points the weekdays are called even rather than ranking noise. The Calendar shades each day by the share of habits kept and shows a dot per completed habit; tapping a day opens a sheet of that day's habits with their checks, for logging or backfilling. The Weekly Recap is a poster like the Year Calendar, shareable as a PNG. Milestones are rings that fill toward each badge. Routines appear as chips above the overview only once one exists, so the overview is unchanged without them. Streak-frozen days use ice blue (`#7DD3FC`) with a snowflake on every grid. The timer is a large ring with SF Mono digits; its Live Activity uses the dark canvas with the habit's color for the clock and Finish button.

## Icon suggestions

Added at the owner's request: the habit icon follows the words in the name. As the name is typed (settling a quarter second after typing pauses, so the icon does not flicker between partial words), a row of up to eight suggested icons appears under the Name field (hidden while nothing matches, so the empty New Habit sheet still matches the reference). The first suggestion becomes the habit's icon and the hero icon swaps with a short scale-and-fade (none with Reduce Motion). Chips are 42-point rounded squares on the field fill with the field hairline; the selected chip takes a 14% habit-color fill and a 60% habit-color outline, like the picker's selection. A leading muted sparkles glyph marks the row as suggestions.

Tapping a chip or choosing in the picker fixes the icon; later name changes only refresh the row. A new habit follows the name until then. An existing habit follows it only while its icon is still the default or the one its name already suggests, so an icon the owner chose is never replaced. Clearing the name restores the icon the editor opened with. A quit habit, or a name with "quit", "no", "less" and similar words, leads with the crossed-out icon (cigarette-off, wine-off, candy-off, phone-off…) or, when Lucide has none, the habit's own icon followed by a ban icon. The picker's search ranks the same matches first, then every other icon whose name or keywords contain the text.

## Compact List Settings

Added at the owner's request from their HabitKit screenshot: tapping the compact list's "Last N days" pill opens a short sheet titled Compact List Settings. Seven 40-point day buttons (1–7) sit 5 points apart; they have hairline outlines and SF Mono digits, and the selected one is filled with the accent. Below them, a Hidden / Shown segmented control sets whether habit names appear. Each choice saves immediately, and the list behind the sheet updates. The title is 22-point bold Figtree, and the two notes are 14-point Figtree in `#A0A0A8`. The sheet uses the field color (`#19191C`) and is 360 points tall, including the home-indicator area. Settings keeps its Compact View Days and Compact Habit Names rows for the same two values.

Added later at the owner's request: a third section, "Configure which habits are hidden everywhere". It holds a 50-point Hidden Habits row shaped like the segmented control above it, with an eye-off icon, the number hidden and a chevron. The sheet grows by 99 points to 459, including the home-indicator area. The row opens **Hide Habits**, a list of every active habit with a check circle (filled with the accent, with a check, when hidden). A tap hides or shows a habit at once, and a Show All text button beside the hidden count under the list shows them all again. Hidden habits leave every screen and widget until they are shown again, and the list behind the sheet updates. Settings has the same Hidden Habits row under Archived Habits, so the list is reachable from any overview layout.

iOS 27 shows a partial-height sheet as a floating card inset 8 points from the screen edges. It lays the sheet out at full width and shrinks it to about 96%. So, as with the large sheets, the contents match the reference relative to the sheet: on the 393-point simulator every element lands within about half a point of the owner's screenshot in sheet coordinates. On screen the sheet sits about 10 points lower and is slightly smaller. iOS also avoids leaving one word alone on a line, so the first note wraps as "…in the / compact list" where the reference wraps as "…in the compact / list".

## Theme and accent color

Added at the owner's request (not in HabitKit): the Theme page in Settings customizes the interface. **Appearance** keeps the System / Light / Dark rows. **Accent Color** starts with a preview row: the filled plus control, a Save capsule and a switch, drawn by the same components the app uses. Below it are a Default row (the reference violet `#8B8AF8`, which new installs and older backups keep), the 21-color habit palette in the editor's 7 × 3 grid, and a Custom row with the system color picker. Rows keep the settings geometry: 53 points high, with a 34-point swatch where other rows have an icon tile. A note under the group says what the accent colors and that each habit keeps its own color.

The accent replaces violet everywhere it meant "the app" rather than a habit: Save and other primary capsules, the plus and other filled round controls, switches, text carets, selected chips, day and weekday buttons, the hidden-habit checks, settings check marks, the all-habits calendar's shading and today ring, the dashboard's "All Habits" and default-color chips, Insights charts for all habits, the welcome title, and multi-habit widgets (Today Ring, Today List, Quick Check, This Week) in the app and on the home screen. Habit colors, single-habit widgets and routine colors are unchanged. Text and glyphs drawn on an accent fill are white, as in the reference, unless the accent is light (WCAG relative luminance above 0.4: orange, amber, yellow, lime, green, mint, teal, cyan, sky or a light custom color), where they are black at 75%, like the completion check on a habit color. Accent-colored text on the light canvas has the same contrast as the habit colors there; light accents are faint as text in Light mode.

A choice applies at once. The color picker saves 0.3 seconds after the color stops changing, or on leaving the page, because dragging reports many colors a second. The open Settings sheet follows a newly chosen appearance at once: iOS keeps a sheet in the scheme it was presented with, and keeps a light or dark one after System is chosen, so Settings takes the overview's scheme explicitly. Alerts, menus, the color picker and the share sheet take the accent through the window tint. Checked on the iOS 27 simulator: the Theme page with the default, amber, teal and a custom crimson accent in both themes, Compact List Settings with mint and New Habit with yellow. Home-screen widgets with a non-default accent have not been checked.

## Touch feedback

Added at the owner's request ("very nice to use"); HabitKit's motion can't be read from screenshots, so these are our choices and change nothing at rest. Buttons with the flat style (check badges, the streak and dashboard checks, compact day tiles and pills, calendar days, Explore tiles, note cards) dim to 70% and press in to 97% while touched, easing out over 0.16 s, so a tap is felt before the screen changes; Reduce Motion keeps the dimming and drops the scale. Streak counts, the week's "done / 7" and the card's streak chip roll to their new value during the check-in animation. A completed check doesn't bounce: checks are tapped many times a day, and the fill, the press and the haptic already confirm it. Touching and holding anywhere on a card, its name and check included, or on a compact row's name, opens the habit's menu over the lifted card or row instead of opening the habit or logging.
